About

WE DON'T
DESIGN.
WE UNLOCK

Unlocked Shot is a visual design studio operating at the intersection of craft and concept. Every project we take on is an opportunity to push against the expected and find the frame that hasn't been captured yet.

Founded by Wilnelson, a self-taught designer with roots in street photography and editorial art, the studio brings a cinematic eye to brand identity, UI design, and visual communication.

We believe design should feel inevitable—as if no other solution could have existed. That conviction shapes everything from our first sketch to the final deliverable.
